# Background Music 🎵

By default, the website has a built-in soothing romantic piano melody synthesized using the browser's Web Audio API (meaning it plays out of the box with zero external files or copyright issues!).

If you have a special song you and Divya love (like your special song or romantic instrumental):
1. Rename your music file to `song.mp3`
2. Place it right here in this `assets/music/` folder: `assets/music/song.mp3`
3. The music player will automatically detect and play your custom song!
